Mowa - App Review Notes

1. ABOUT THE APP
Mowa detects a walk in the background and turns it into an AI-written diary entry.
The user interface is Korean only.

2. DEMO ACCOUNT
Sign-in is required. This beta build has no sign-up: accounts are issued by the
operator. Please use the demo account provided in the review information fields.

3. HOW TO REVIEW WITHOUT WALKING OUTSIDE
The main flow normally begins when the device detects real walking, which is hard
to reproduce indoors. The build includes a developer diagnostics screen that
simulates a completed walk from start to finish:

Step 1. Sign in with the demo account.
Step 2. A permission screen appears. You may allow or skip every permission.
        The simulated flow below works either way.
Step 3. Open the Settings tab, scroll to the bottom, and tap the last item,
        "개발자 진단 열기" (Open developer diagnostics).
Step 4. Scroll down to the section titled "6 · Candidate flow" and tap the button
        "Synthetic FINISHED walk (20 min, with end)".
Step 5. Return to the Home tab. A walk card is now waiting there. Tap it to open
        the recording flow, choose a companion, one or more emotions, and a
        situation, then tap the button that generates the diary with AI.
Step 6. Save the generated diary. It can then be viewed, edited and deleted from
        the Archive tab.

4. WHY THE APP REQUESTS ALWAYS LOCATION AND USES BACKGROUND MODES
Walk detection is the core feature and has to keep running while the app is not in
the foreground. Without it, a walk is recorded only if the user happens to open the
app while walking, which defeats the purpose of the product. Location is used for
two things: keeping the detector alive in the background, and turning the walk's
coordinates into a neighbourhood name such as 망원동 (Mangwon-dong). The raw
coordinates never leave the device. Only the neighbourhood name is sent to our
server. The app does not track users and contains no advertising or analytics SDKs.

5. WHY THE APP REQUESTS HEALTHKIT
The app reads step count and walking distance to corroborate walk detection and to
display step totals to the user. Access is read-only. The app never writes to
HealthKit, never sends health data to any server, and never uses health data for
advertising or marketing.

6. NOTIFICATIONS
All notifications are local. The app has no remote push and no aps-environment
entitlement.
